How a job with us works

From pricing the job to cleaning up after it, here’s what to expect when we work on your home.

  1. Step 1At your house

    Free estimate

    We meet you at your house, go over what needs doing and price the job for you, free.

  2. Step 2A day that works for you

    Scheduling

    When you’re ready, we set a day for the job that works for you.

  3. Step 3On the day we set

    The job

    On the day we set, our crew shows up, does the job as quoted and cleans up before leaving.
