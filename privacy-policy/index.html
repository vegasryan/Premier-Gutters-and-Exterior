Privacy policy

We value your privacy. Here’s what we collect, how we use it and how we keep it safe.

1. What we collect

Our estimate form asks for:

  • your name and phone number
  • your email address, if you give it
  • your address or town, if you give it
  • the service you need, such as new gutters or a repair

When you send it, Netlify, the company that hosts this site, also records the date and time, your device’s internet (IP) address, the browser you use and the page you sent it from.

2. How we use it

We use what you send to contact you about your estimate:

  • to call or email you back
  • to set a time to come out and look at the job
  • to give you a price
  • to keep in touch about the work, if you hire us

We don’t sell your information, and we don’t share it with other companies for their marketing.

3. Cookies and tracking

This site doesn’t use cookies, and it has no tracking or advertising tools on it.

4. The services that run the site

Netlify hosts this site and stores the forms you send for us. It checks each form for spam with Akismet, a spam filter.

The photos, the fonts and the office map all load from this site too, not from Google or any other company.

Some links lead to other companies’ sites, such as Google Maps (the Get directions button), our Google reviews, and our Facebook and Instagram pages. Those companies only hear from you if you click, and their own privacy policies apply there.

5. Keeping it safe

We take reasonable steps to keep what you send us safe. The site uses a secure (https) connection, so the form is encrypted on its way to Netlify. Still, nothing sent over the internet is completely secure.

6. Your rights

You can contact us at any time to ask what we have about you, or to ask us to delete it.

You also choose what to send. We only need your name, phone number and the service. Your email and address are up to you.

7. How to reach us

If you have a question about this policy, call or email us.

  • By phone: 806-829-2622, Mon to Fri, 9am to 5pm
  • By email: info@premierguttersandexterior.com
  • At our office: 8228 Valencia Ave, Lubbock, TX 79424

By using this site, you agree to this policy. What you read on this site is general information only.
