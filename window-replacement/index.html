FAQs

Window replacement questions

What is the average cost to replace a window in Lubbock?

It depends on the window’s size and style, the frame and the glass, and whether the opening needs any repair, so an average says little about your house. Call us and we’ll measure your windows and give you a price before any work starts.

Is it cheaper to buy windows and have someone install them?

The window can cost less that way, but then the measuring and ordering are on you, and a window that’s even a little off won’t fit the opening. When one company measures, orders and installs, getting it right is that company’s job. We measure your windows at your house before anything is ordered, so each new window fits its opening.

Do I need a permit to replace windows in Lubbock?

It depends on the job. A window replaced in the same opening is simpler than cutting a new opening or making one bigger, which changes the wall. The City of Lubbock can tell you what your job needs. Call us and we’ll go over what yours involves.

What is the cheapest time of year to replace windows?

There isn’t one cheap month. The price comes mostly from the windows themselves: the kind, the size and how many you need. Installers are busiest right after a big hailstorm, when many homes need windows at once. The estimate is free, so you can get a price whenever you’re ready.

How long do vinyl windows last?

Well-made vinyl windows can last for decades. What wears them out in Lubbock is strong sun, which can fade and warp the frames, and hail, which can crack the glass. How well they’re sealed when they go in matters too: a gap around the frame lets in water and air and shortens their life.

Is Lowe’s or Home Depot better for windows?

Either can be a good place to buy a window. What a local installer does differently is take on the whole job: one company measures, orders and installs, and if a question comes up later, you call the same office. Ours is on Valencia Ave in Lubbock, and the estimate at your house is free.
