What a gutter cleaning includes

Here’s what we take care of on a cleaning, from the roof edge down to the yard.

Gutters cleared

Inside each gutter, we clear out the leaves and dirt so rain runs to the downspouts again.

Black streaks on the outside of gutters

Dark stripes down the front of your gutters are common, and they can usually be cleaned off.

What the streaks are
The gray-black stuff on the face and underside of your gutters is mostly dirt, soot and grime the rain washes off your roof, with mildew where it stays damp.
Where they form
Streaks form where water runs over the gutter’s outer edge and down its face. That often happens when a clogged gutter overflows.
How they come off
They generally come off with a cleaner made for the job, a soft brush and a rinse. Hard scrubbing or high pressure can scratch the finish.
Keeping new ones away
A clear gutter that doesn’t overflow helps keep new streaks from forming. Have streaks on yours? Ask about them at your free estimate, or call us.

Clean less often with gutter guards

Gutter guards cover the top of your gutters and keep out most of the leaves, pine needles and other debris that clog them. We install roll-lock steel, stainless-steel mesh and louvered guards.
