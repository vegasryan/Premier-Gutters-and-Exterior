What hail does to gutters, windows, fascia and siding

After a hailstorm, most people check the roof first. But the same hail dents gutters and downspouts and cracks window glass, fascia and siding. Each card below covers one part of your house: what hail does to it, how we fix it, and a link to that service.

  • Gutter repair Hail dents gutters and downspouts. We repair the damage, or replace a run with a new seamless gutter formed on site. Learn more
  • Window replacement Hail breaks window glass and tears screens. We measure and install new windows. Learn more
  • Fascia and soffit repair Hail can dent and crack fascia, the board your gutters hang on. We replace damaged fascia, so your gutters stay firmly attached. Learn more
  • Siding Hail can crack siding, and strong wind can damage it too. We repair or replace it in vinyl, engineered wood or metal. Learn more

Help with your insurance claim

After a hailstorm, the repair often goes through your homeowners insurance. Here’s what to expect, and where we come in.

Does insurance cover hail damage?

Often, but it depends on your policy. Many homeowners policies cover hail damage, less your deductible, and some have a separate deductible for wind and hail. Only your insurance company can say what your claim will pay, so we never promise an amount.

We can help with insurance claims

Our office in Lubbock helps you work with your insurance company. We look over the damage, take photos of it, and write an itemized estimate you can send them. If the adjuster has questions about the repair, we answer them. Call us to talk through your claim.
