Gutters

New seamless gutters for Lubbock homes, and the repairs, cleaning and guards that keep them working.

  • Gutter installation Seamless aluminum gutters, formed on site from coil milled in the US, so each run is one piece with no seams along its length. Choose K-style in 5 or 6 inch, half-round or box, in a color that suits your house. Learn more
  • Gutter repair Leaking seams, sagging runs, loose downspouts and hail dents, fixed by our local crew. When a gutter sags because the fascia behind it has rotted, we can replace the fascia too. Learn more
  • Gutter cleaning Gutters and downspouts cleaned out, so rain runs off your roof and away from your house. A clogged gutter holds water against the fascia board until the wood goes soft. Learn more
  • Gutter guards Guards keep leaves and debris out, so your gutters stay clear between cleanings. We install roll-lock powder-coated steel, stainless-steel mesh and louvered guards, and we can cover downspouts too. Learn more

Exterior

Fascia, siding and windows for Lubbock homes, from the same local company that does your gutters.

  • Fascia and soffit repair Fascia is the board along your roof’s edge that your gutters hang on. When it rots, cracks or warps, we replace it in wood, aluminum-wrapped wood or composite board, often with new gutters. Learn more
  • Siding New siding in vinyl, engineered wood or metal, and siding repairs after hail and wind. Siding is the layer that shields your walls from the weather and from insects. Learn more
  • Window replacement Replacement windows, measured and installed by our local crew, including windows broken by hail. When hail is the cause, our office will help you work with your insurance company. Learn more
  • Window repair Broken glass, foggy windows and torn screens fixed on Lubbock homes, often after hail. At a free estimate we’ll tell you whether a repair will do or a new window is the better fix. Learn more

Storm damage

Repairs after a Lubbock hailstorm, with help on your insurance claim.

  • Hail damage repair Hail can dent gutters, break windows and crack fascia and siding. We repair and replace all of it, and the estimate is free. Learn more
