FAQs

Siding questions

Do I need a permit to replace siding in Lubbock?

It depends on the job and on the city’s rules. Replacing siding board for board is a different job from work on the wall behind it, and the City of Lubbock can tell you what yours needs. Call us and we’ll go over what your job involves.

What is the best time of year to install siding?

Siding can go up most of the year, but mild spring and fall days are easiest on the crew and the materials. Vinyl gets stiff in hard cold and grows a little in summer heat, so it’s hung with room to move in any season. If hail has cracked or holed your siding, don’t wait for a better month: water can get in behind it.

Which siding holds up best to hail?

Each has its trade-offs. Metal won’t crack but can dent, vinyl can crack or chip from big hail, especially in cold weather, and engineered wood is tougher than vinyl but needs paint. Thicker panels of any kind stand up better. Call us and we’ll talk through which suits your house.

Can you replace just a few damaged panels?

Often, if matching panels can still be found. Siding fades in the sun, so new panels can look brighter than the old ones for a while, and some older styles aren’t made anymore. Call us and we’ll see what can be matched.

How much does new siding cost?

It depends on the size of the house, the siding you pick, how much trim and how many corners there are, and whether the old siding has to come off first. Engineered wood and metal usually cost more than vinyl. Call us and we’ll give you a price before any work starts.
