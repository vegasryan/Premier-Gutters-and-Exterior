Replacing old siding

Here’s how to tell when it’s time for siding replacement, and where to look on your house.

Cracks and holes

After a hailstorm, look over every wall. Cracks and holes let the rain in.

Warping or buckling

Look along each wall from one corner. Wavy or bulging panels have warped with the heat and cold.

Loose or missing panels

Look up at the siding on a windy day. Panels that rattle or lift have come loose from the wall.

Faded, chalky color

Check the sunniest walls. Faded or chalky color means the siding is worn from weather and sun.

Soft spots or bubbling paint

Check wood siding near the ground. Soft spots or bubbling paint mean water is trapped behind it.
